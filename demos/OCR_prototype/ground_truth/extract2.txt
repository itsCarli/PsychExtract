I saw my best friend cry for the first time, and I don’t know how to handle it
Today I saw my best friend completely break down. He’s always been the strong, funny one who keeps everyone else together, so when he started crying in front of me it didn’t even feel real. I froze. I didn’t know what to say or do, and I feel guilty for not comforting him the way he’s always comforted me. It made me realize how much pain he must’ve been hiding all this time.
