Something that keeps bothering me even if I tell myself it doesn’t matter
How does it feel to be no one’s favourite?

I have friends, but I don’t think I’m anyone’s best friend. I have cousins and relatives, but they clearly love and feel more attached to my older sister. I’m not even close to being anyone’s favourite....that feels like a very distant, unreachable thing for me.

The people around me feel emotionally distant. I can’t even express how I feel without overthinking everything. Over time, I’ve developed so many trust issues that I keep wondering if people are just using me or treating me like a time pass.

I don’t think I’m asking for too much. I just want someone who chooses me, someone who wants me as their best friend, someone who actually stays. Is that really too much to ask?

Honestly, I don’t even understand these feelings. I just know they hurt.
